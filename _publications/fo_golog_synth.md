abstract: "We investigate the problem of synthesizing strategies that guarantee the successful execution of a high-level nondeterministic agent program &delta; in Golog within a nondeterministic first-order basic action theory, considering the environment as adversarial. Our approach constructs a symbolic program graph that captures the control flow of &delta; independently of the domain, enabling strategy synthesis through the cross product of the program graph with the domain model. We formally relate graph-based transitions to standard Golog semantics and provide a synthesis procedure that is sound though incomplete (in general, the problem is undecidable, given that we have a first-order representation of the state). We also extend the framework to handle the case where the environment's possible behaviors are specified by a Golog program."
