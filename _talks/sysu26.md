abstract: 'This seminar presents an overview of selected research topics in symbolic and neuro-symbolic artificial intelligence. I will discuss recent work on reasoning and planning in dynamic and nondeterministic domains, focusing on logical formalisms such as the Situation Calculus and Golog for strategy synthesis and controller construction with formal guarantees. I will also introduce a complementary line of research on neuro-symbolic predictive process monitoring, where neural sequence models are integrated with temporal logic constraints to ensure logically consistent predictions. The talk aims to provide a coherent overview of ongoing research efforts and future directions.'
